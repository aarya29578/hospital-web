Musculoskeletal Diagnosis Musculoskeletal Diagnosis At Mumbai Ortho Clinics , we offer comprehensive diagnostic evaluation for all musculoskeletal conditions, including joint disorders, muscle injuries, ligament tears, tendon issues, bone problems, and chronic pain. Our goal is to identify the root cause of pain with precision and provide a clear treatment plan tailored to each patient. What We Diagnose Joint Pain & Degenerative Disorders Conditions such as arthritis, cartilage wear, inflammation, and joint instability are assessed using advanced tools to understand their severity and impact. Muscle & Soft Tissue Injuries Strains, tears, spasms, and overuse injuries are evaluated through clinical tests and imaging to determine the appropriate treatment. Ligament & Tendon Problems Sports injuries, ligament sprains, tendonitis, and partial tears are diagnosed to prevent long-term instability or mobility issues. Bone & Structural Abnormalities We assess fractures, bone deformities, alignment issues, and stress injuries affecting functionality and movement. Nerve-Related Musculoskeletal Pain Issues such as nerve compression, radiating pain, numbness, and tingling are diagnosed to determine whether they originate from the spine, joints, or soft tissues. Diagnostic Methods We Use Physical Examination Detailed movement testing, strength assessment, and pain mapping to understand the condition clinically. Digital X-Rays High-clarity imaging to detect fractures, joint space narrowing, deformities, and bone conditions. Ultrasound Scanning Real-time imaging used to evaluate soft tissue concerns like tendon injuries, fluid buildup, or inflammation. MRI (If Required) When deeper evaluation is needed, MRI scans help diagnose ligament tears, cartilage damage, and complex injuries.
